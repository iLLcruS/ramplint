# RL305 `definition-list`

<dl> may only contain <dt>, <dd> and <div> groups of them.

## WCAG

- [1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html) (level A)

## Why it matters

A description list connects terms with their descriptions: "Weight: 2 kg", "Colour: black". Screen readers announce that structure only when `<dt>` and `<dd>` sit directly inside a `<dl>`, optionally grouped in a `<div>`. Other elements break the list, and a `<dt>` / `<dd>` outside a `<dl>` is read as plain text.

## What ramplint reports

- Direct children of `<dl>` other than `<dt>`, `<dd>`, `<div>`, `<script>` and `<template>`.
- Children of a `<div>` group inside `<dl>` other than `<dt>`, `<dd>`, `<script>` and `<template>`.
- `<dt>` and `<dd>` that are not inside a `<dl>`.
- A `<dd>` with no `<dt>` before it and a `<dt>` with no `<dd>` after it, in the `<dl>` or in a `<div>` group. Each group is one or more terms followed by one or more descriptions.
- An empty `<dt>`.

## Examples

Problems:

```django
{# ✗ #} <dl class="specs">{% for spec in specs %}<p><dt>{{ spec.name }}</dt><dd>{{ spec.value }}</dd></p>{% endfor %}</dl>
{# ✗ #} <dl><div class="row"><span>{{ spec.name }}</span><dd>{{ spec.value }}</dd></div></dl>
{# ✗ #} <section class="specs"><dt>{% trans "Weight" %}</dt><dd>{{ product.weight }}</dd></section>
{# ✗ #} <dl><dt>{% trans "Weight" %}</dt><div><dd>{{ product.weight }}</dd></div></dl>
{# ✗ #} <dl><dt></dt><dd>{{ product.weight }}</dd></dl>
```

Fixed:

```django
{# ✓ #} <dl class="specs">{% for spec in specs %}<dt>{{ spec.name }}</dt><dd>{{ spec.value }}</dd>{% endfor %}</dl>
{# ✓ #} <dl><div class="row"><dt>{{ spec.name }}</dt><dd>{{ spec.value }}</dd></div></dl>
{# ✓ #} <section class="specs"><dl><dt>{% trans "Weight" %}</dt><dd>{{ product.weight }}</dd></dl></section>
{# ✓ #} <dl><div><dt>{% trans "Weight" %}</dt><dd>{{ product.weight }}</dd></div></dl>
```

## Not reported

- Lists whose content comes from `{% include %}`, `{% block %}` or a variable: a missing `<dt>` or `<dd>` may be rendered there.
- `<dt>` / `<dd>` (or a `<div>` group of them) at the top level of a partial template: it is included into a `<dl>` elsewhere. Full-page templates (with an `<html>` element) are still checked.
- `<dl>` with a `role`, and elements with dynamic attributes.
- Content of `<template>`.

## Suppressing

```django
{# ramplint: ignore RL305 #}
```

on the line with the problem or the line above it, or for the whole file:

```django
{# ramplint: ignore-file RL305 #}
```

You can also disable the rule in `pyproject.toml`:

```toml
[tool.ramplint]
ignore = ["RL305"]
```
