# RL802 `target-size`

Clickable targets must be at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels, or have room around them.

## WCAG

- [2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) (level AA)

## Why it matters

Small targets that sit close together are hard to hit for people with tremors, limited dexterity or a large finger on a small screen, and they cause mistaken clicks.

## What ramplint reports

This rule needs a rendered page: it runs in the browser scan
(`ramplint http://localhost:8000/` or `ramplint --runserver`) and is skipped
when ramplint lints templates.

- Links, buttons, form controls and elements with an interactive role that are smaller than 24 by 24 CSS pixels **and** too close to another target: a 24 px circle centred on the small target touches another target (or the circle of another small target).

## Examples

Problems:

```html
<button style="width:16px;height:16px" aria-label="Previous"></button><button style="width:16px;height:16px" aria-label="Next"></button>
```

Fixed:

```html
<button style="width:24px;height:24px" aria-label="Previous"></button><button style="width:24px;height:24px" aria-label="Next"></button>
<button style="width:16px;height:16px;margin:12px" aria-label="Close"></button>
```

## Not reported

- Small targets with enough space around them (the spacing exception).
- Links inside a sentence or a paragraph (the inline exception).
- Checkboxes and radio buttons with a label: the label is clickable too.
- Form controls left at the browser's default style (WCAG exempts targets whose size the user agent determines).

## Suppressing

Put an HTML comment (it stays in the rendered page) on the line with the
element or the line above it:

```html
<!-- ramplint: ignore RL802 -->
```

or disable the rule in `pyproject.toml`:

```toml
[tool.ramplint]
ignore = ["RL802"]
```
