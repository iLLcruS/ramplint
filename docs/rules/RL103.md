# RL103 `video-caption`

Videos must have captions.

## WCAG

- [1.2.2 Captions (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html) (level A)

## Why it matters

Deaf and hard-of-hearing users cannot follow speech, music or sound effects without captions, and many people watch with the sound off. Captions are added with a `<track>` element pointing to a WebVTT file.

## What ramplint reports

- `<video>` without `<track kind="captions">` or `<track kind="subtitles">`. A `<track>` without `kind` counts as subtitles; `descriptions`, `chapters` and `metadata` tracks do not count.

## Examples

Problems:

```django
{# ✗ #} <video controls src="{{ product.video.url }}"></video>
{# ✗ #} <video controls><source src="{% static 'video/intro.mp4' %}" type="video/mp4"><track kind="chapters" src="{% static 'video/intro.chapters.vtt' %}"></video>
```

Fixed:

```django
{# ✓ #} <video controls src="{{ product.video.url }}"><track kind="captions" src="{{ product.captions.url }}" srclang="en" label="English"></video>
{# ✓ #} <video autoplay muted loop playsinline src="{% static 'video/background.mp4' %}"></video>
```

## Not reported

- `<video muted>`: a silent background video has nothing to caption.
- Videos whose tracks come from `{% include %}` or a variable placed right inside `<video>`, and tracks with a dynamic `kind`.
- Videos with dynamic attributes or `aria-hidden="true"`, and content of `<template>`.

ramplint cannot tell whether a video has sound or whether the captions are correct; check those by hand. Videos embedded with `<iframe>` (YouTube, Vimeo) need captions on the hosting service.

## Suppressing

```django
{# ramplint: ignore RL103 #}
```

on the line with the problem or the line above it, or for the whole file:

```django
{# ramplint: ignore-file RL103 #}
```

You can also disable the rule in `pyproject.toml`:

```toml
[tool.ramplint]
ignore = ["RL103"]
```
