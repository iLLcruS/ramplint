# RL801 `color-contrast`

Text must have a contrast of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).

## WCAG

- [1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) (level AA)

## Why it matters

People with low vision, colour blindness or a screen in sunlight cannot read text that is too close to its background colour. WCAG asks for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 18.66 px and bold).

## What ramplint reports

This rule needs a rendered page: it runs in the browser scan
(`ramplint http://localhost:8000/` or `ramplint --runserver`) and is skipped
when ramplint lints templates.

- Text whose colour, painted over every background colour behind it (semi-transparent backgrounds and the element's opacity included), has a contrast ratio below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).
- The message gives the ratio and the two colours, e.g. `Contrast of 2.32:1 is below 4.5:1 for text (#aaaaaa on #ffffff)`.
- Text over a background image or a gradient cannot be measured from styles: it is listed as **needs a manual check** in the report, not as a problem.
- Menus and dialogs opened during the scan are checked too.

## Examples

Problems:

```html
<p style="color: #aaa">Light grey on white</p>
<div style="background: rgba(0, 0, 0, .5)"><span style="color: #222">Dark on half-black</span></div>
```

Fixed:

```html
<p style="color: #595959">Dark grey on white (7:1)</p>
<h2 style="color: #949494; font-size: 24px">Large text needs 3:1</h2>
```

## Not reported

- Disabled controls (`disabled`, `aria-disabled="true"`): WCAG exempts inactive components.
- Hidden text and text with `opacity: 0`.
- Logos and text in images (they are images, see RL101).

## Suppressing

Put an HTML comment (it stays in the rendered page) on the line with the
element or the line above it:

```html
<!-- ramplint: ignore RL801 -->
```

or disable the rule in `pyproject.toml`:

```toml
[tool.ramplint]
ignore = ["RL801"]
```
