# Commercial license

ramplint is free for individuals, learning, research, non-profits and small
companies. Larger companies need a commercial license.

## Do I need one?

| Who uses ramplint | License |
|-------------------|---------|
| You, for personal projects, learning or research | Free ([PolyForm Noncommercial](LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md)) |
| A school, charity, public research or government institution | Free ([PolyForm Noncommercial](LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md)) |
| A company with fewer than 100 people **and** less than 1,000,000 USD revenue in the prior tax year | Free ([PolyForm Small Business](LICENSES/PolyForm-Small-Business-1.0.0.md)) |
| Any other company, in any way (on developers' machines, in CI, in pre-commit hooks) | **Commercial license** |

"People" means employees and independent contractors together, and the
company includes the organizations that control it or that it controls. The
license texts in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/) are what counts; this table is a
summary.

Versions 0.5.0 and earlier were released under the MIT License and stay
available under it.

## How to get one

Write to [rostovskii.1020@gmail.com](mailto:rostovskii.1020@gmail.com) with:

- the company name and country,
- roughly how many developers will use ramplint,
- whether you need an invoice.

You get a written license for your company, usually within a few days.
Pricing is per company and depends on its size. If you are not sure whether
you need a license, just ask.
